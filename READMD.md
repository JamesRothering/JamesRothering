## Fractional CTO / Enterprise Architect

I work at the point where things are supposed to be stuck.

Legacy systems, political constraints, aggressive timelines, regulatory pressure—situations where the default answer is usually “this can’t be done right now.” Those are the environments I’ve spent most of my career in.

I bridge business intent and technical execution, and I don’t stop at architecture diagrams. I’ll go as far down the stack as needed—code, infrastructure, data—whatever creates forward motion.

I’ve led zero-downtime bank migrations, stabilized high-scale systems under load, and built workable paths forward when teams were blocked by things that weren’t going to change.

---

## How I Work

* **Constraints are part of the system**
  If access is blocked or dependencies are frozen, I don’t wait—I design around them

* **Create motion early**
  A partial but working system beats a perfect design that hasn’t started

* **Use working software to align people**
  A prototype will resolve more debate than a slide deck

* **Go deep when it matters**
  I’ll step into code or infrastructure directly if that’s the fastest way to get clarity

* **Connect business intent to technical reality**
  Systems fail when those drift apart—I keep them aligned
