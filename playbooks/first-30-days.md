## What I Do in My First 30 Days as a Fractional CTO

Most environments don’t need more ideas. They need clarity and movement.

The first 30 days are about understanding what matters, identifying what’s actually in the way, and getting something real into motion.

---

### Week 1: Understand the System

* Understand business priorities
* Map the current architecture (infrastructure, data, services)
* Identify critical dependencies and failure points
* Talk to engineers and business owners to understand where things actually break

---

### Week 2: Identify Risk and Constraints

* Find the real bottlenecks in delivery and scalability
* Surface technical debt that is actively slowing the system down
* Identify where business intent and system behavior are out of sync

---

### Week 3: Define Direction

* Establish a practical target architecture (not theoretical)
* Make clear build vs buy decisions where needed
* Identify a small number of changes that will create immediate momentum

---

### Week 4: Start Execution

* Deliver initial improvements
* Align the team around a clear path forward
* Put basic metrics in place so the system can be observed and trusted

---

## Principles Behind This

* Start with what matters to the business
* Don’t wait for perfect conditions
* Make systems visible before trying to scale them
* Prefer simple systems that can evolve over complex ones that stall

---

## Expected Outcome

By the end of 30 days:

* The important problems are visible
* The system is better understood
* The team is moving again
* There is a clear direction that connects business goals to technical execution
